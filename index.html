Message From The Founder

THE MAN WITH A VISION

A Journey of Resilience, Passion & Purpose

The Journey Began in 2011

Every great vision begins with a humble step. My journey in the hardware industry started in Mumbai in 2011, with a simple job, a passion for learning, and a determination to create something meaningful. Beginning in retail and later moving into wholesale, I dedicated myself to understanding every aspect of the industry.

Over the years, I travelled extensively across India, building strong relationships with customers, manufacturers, and businesses — from the manufacturing hubs of Rajkot and Jamnagar to markets across the nation.

Turning Setbacks into Strength

My journey was not without challenges. An early business venture ended in financial losses, but it taught me lessons that success alone could never have offered. Instead of giving up, I chose to learn from my mistakes, rebuild with greater determination, and continue moving forward.

The Birth of ELVY HARDWARE • 2025

After more than a decade in Mumbai, I moved to Bengaluru in 2023, bringing with me extensive industry experience and strong connections across South India. In 2025, I founded ELVY HARDWARE — a brand born from years of experience, creative passion, and a clear vision to redefine hardware through quality, design, and genuine customer relationships.

As a founder and graphic designer, I remain personally involved in product selection, design, branding, marketing, and management. Every collection reflects my belief that exceptional hardware should combine aesthetic elegance, reliable performance, and lasting value.

“I didn’t just build a brand. I turned my vision into a legacy.”

Bhavesh Singh Founder • ELVY HARDWARE Bengaluru, India • Established 2025
